### **Overview**
The **Arizona Nutrition Aid** is a high-fidelity Clinical Decision Support (CDS) tool designed for RDN candidates and students at **The University of Arizona**. By utilizing the **Mission Loop** framework, the tool helps practitioners identify dietary **Patterns**, apply clinical **Rules**, and generate documented **Solves**.

---

### **Core Framework: The Mission Loop**
1. **Pattern (Assessment):** Identify patient anthropometrics and usual intake.
2. **Rule (Audit):** Apply the **DGA 14g/1000kcal Fiber Rule** and **Hamwi IBW** standards.
3. **Solve (ADIME):** Generate a standardized PES statement and ADIME note.

### **Clinical Authority (SSoT)**
* **Fiber Density:** 14g per 1000 kcal per the **U.S. Dietary Guidelines for Americans (DGA)**.
* **Ideal Body Weight:** Calculated via the **Hamwi Method** (Standard Clinical Baseline).

---

### **Usage Instructions**
1. Input client height and gender in the **Assessment Box**.
2. Input reported energy and fiber intake in the **Nutrient Auditor**.
3. Review the generated PES statement in the **ADIME Box**.
4. Click **"Copy Note"** to transfer the audited findings to your clinical record.

---

### **Clinical Disclaimer**
> **NOTICE:** This tool provides AI-enhanced clinical drafts. **Users are responsible for the final verification of all results.** All data must be audited against the primary SSoT (DGA/Hamwi) before implementation in a clinical setting.
